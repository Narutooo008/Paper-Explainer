Pipeline

How PaperExplainer works

STEP 1
Extract & Filter

PyMuPDF extracts core sections and figures, filtering out related work, bibliographies, and appendix noise.

STEP 2
Script & Diagrams

NVIDIA LLM crafts an intuitive script and designs animated diagrams (flowcharts, cycles, bar charts, comparisons).

STEP 3
Narrate & Animate

Edge-TTS records natural speech while Pillow and FFmpeg stream 24 fps ease-out scenes into a high-res MP4.
