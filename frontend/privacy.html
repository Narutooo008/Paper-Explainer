Privacy & Data Policy

PaperExplainer is built with a local-first architecture. This document details precisely what data is processed, what is stored on your machine, and how third-party APIs are contacted.

1. Local Storage on Your Device

All core processing artifacts are retained solely on your machine in the local jobs/ folder within the application root directory:

  • Uploaded PDFs: The original PDF uploaded via file drop or retrieved from arXiv is saved locally in jobs/{job_id}/paper.pdf.
  • Extracted text and cached summaries: Raw text extracted by PyMuPDF and condensed model summaries are kept in jobs/{job_id}/cache/ to accelerate repeat runs.
  • Generated Media: Audio clips (.mp3), rendered frames (.png), final 24 fps MP4 videos, .srt/.vtt subtitle files, and study notes (paper-notes.md) reside only on your drive.

2. Third-Party Transmission

To produce summaries, storyboards, and speech narration, PaperExplainer makes encrypted HTTPS requests to external provider APIs:

  • NVIDIA NIM API: Text chunks extracted from the paper are sent to your configured model endpoint (at integrate.api.nvidia.com) using the API key specified in your local .env file. No personal profile data or user identity is sent.
  • Microsoft Edge-TTS: Spoken sentences from the generated script are passed to Microsoft's Edge-TTS service over HTTPS to synthesize natural speech.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: PaperExplainer includes zero tracking pixels, zero analytics scripts, zero advertisement beacons, and zero third-party cookie trackers.

3. Data Deletion & Control

You have full, permanent control over all stored data:

  • One-Click In-App Deletion: On the home page, clicking the "Delete" button next to any previous paper immediately deletes the entire job folder from disk.
  • Manual Purge: You can delete the entire jobs/ folder at any time in your filesystem; the application will automatically recreate an empty directory upon restart.
