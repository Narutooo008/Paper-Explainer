About PaperExplainer

PaperExplainer is a free, open-source tool designed to make academic literature more digestible by transforming dense research papers into animated 24 fps video explainers, study notes, and interactive Q&A sessions.

Why PaperExplainer?

Academic literature moves at an unprecedented pace. Thousands of preprints are submitted to arXiv weekly across artificial intelligence, computer systems, and engineering. However, unpacking the core contributions, methodologies, and limitations of a 20-page manuscript typically demands hours of concentrated reading.

PaperExplainer bridges this gap: it extracts the fundamental argument, synthesizes animated diagrams (such as flowcharts, loops, and comparisons), and generates audio narration synced to high-resolution slides.

Local-First Philosophy

Unlike hosted AI dashboards that store uploaded documents in proprietary cloud accounts, PaperExplainer runs local-first:

  • Your device, your files: Uploaded PDFs, speech audio, animation frames, and study notes remain stored exclusively inside your local jobs/ folder.
  • Instant cleanup: Completed jobs can be completely purged with a single click or by clearing the folder.
  • Independent execution: The server runs on your machine via FastAPI, Uvicorn, and Python, requiring no external subscription.

The Free AI Tier (NVIDIA NIM)

PaperExplainer utilizes NVIDIA's developer API tier (build.nvidia.com). Developers receive free trial credits to access high-performance open models like Llama 3.2 and Nemotron directly via standard OpenAI-compatible API calls.

Configuring your key: Get an API key from build.nvidia.com, add it to your .env file under NVIDIA_API_KEY, and PaperExplainer handles the rest—including automatic model fallback and startup validation.
